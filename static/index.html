    Only the source you select is shared. A check sends a JPEG snapshot and your optional corner crop to this local server and local Ollama. This app makes no screen recording, saves no frames or live advice, and captures no audio. Stop sharing ends capture and discards later replies; an analysis already started in Ollama may finish locally. The model can misread the screen and cannot know hidden enemies, your full plan, or events between snapshots. Review suggestions with that context.

    ⓘ

    Useful coaching starts with honest evidence.

    The dashboard uses transparent coaching rules, not a trained model or a comparison with Divine players. Optional report chat interprets the loaded statistics and does not inspect game images. The separate live screen coach reviews selected snapshots with a local vision model; it does not understand a full replay or the continuous match. Neither can guarantee exact positioning judgments or prove why a fight was lost. Targets are starting exercises and depend on your role, hero, matchup, and patch. No rank gain is guaranteed.

    Ranked only for match-history coaching. Remakes under 10 minutes and matches this account abandoned are excluded. Safe lane does not automatically mean carry. A manual role is your assumption for all matches in that review. The in-game replay remains the source of context.

    Data goes from OpenDota to a local Python server. Live screen snapshots go only to the selected local vision workflow. No Steam login, uploads to a cloud AI service, telemetry, or CDN assets. Reports and cache may contain public account information; share exports deliberately.
